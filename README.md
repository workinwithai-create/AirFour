# AirFour

Four-bar air desk for bedroom producers and AI-music finishers.

Suno and loop DAWs stay dense or fade the file. Session players write four live bars of strategic space — kit thins, chairs hold or drop, air appears — so the next section hits with room. Then the money lands.

Live samples (FluidR3 piano, upright, nylon, trumpet, violin, acoustic kit) loaded from the PreEight sample bank. No oscillators.

Yesterday's ships were FingerFour / BassFour (finger-bass under unfinished loops and choruses). AirFour is the missing space before the hit.

## Distinct from the rest of the line

| Tool | Job |
| --- | --- |
| TagFour | Four-bar *last-line tag* that ends the record |
| LiftTwo | Two bars of climb *into* the hook |
| PreEight | Eight bars *before* the hook |
| AfterHook | Eight bars *after* the hook as a floor / vamp |
| EndEight | Last eight bars of the record |
| LastHook | Last chorus is the money chorus |
| BreakFour | Four-bar live breakdown |
| StopFour | Stop-time figures |
| **AirFour** | Four bars of **strategic air** so the next section has room to hit |

## Loop

1. Pick a groove (Amber Walk, Porch Climb, Fold Radio, Stair House, Carbon Verse).
2. Hear A: the dense loop. Eight bars. It loops.
3. Stamp an air recipe (kit out, pedal only, one chair, half air, hold swell…).
4. Hear B: eight of dense, four of air, then it stops.
5. Copy the punch list.

Space plays B. A is dense. 8 is air only.
